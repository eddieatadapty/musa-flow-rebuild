# Musa Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/musa-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Musa users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Musa's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Show her cycle before you show the price | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Let her earn day 1 before the paywall | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Put the free week on a timeline | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Make the yearly saving impossible to miss | +10–15% ARPU (annual mix) |
| 05 | Let her say no, then offer a smaller yes | +10–15% ARPU |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Musa's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Musa. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
